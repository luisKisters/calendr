# Calendr v14: busy hours without "+N"

Replaces design/mockup-v13 (superseded). Scope: only the time-grid "+N" chip goes. The all-day "+N more" and the month "N more" stay.
The user said: "yourt examples are perhaps a bit etreme … create actually realisitc scnerarios many of them and gimme actual things i can adjust and change an argue your decisions".

## Locked in round 1
- 2 or 3 events at the same time: smart, as locked (the v11 cascade).
- A crowded spot: side by side, equal days, titles wrap (not the recommendation).
- Crowded from: 5 at once. Other knobs at their defaults: title lines in a narrow event 3.
- The user said: "also make sure that for example here the time could still be shown but isnt" (a tall cascaded block, "Familiene…", one line and no time). Rule: a block plans its text on the part that shows, up to the first later block that covers it, so the title wraps and the time shows when there is room. This replaces "time in narrow events: hidden".

## Round 1 options (for the record)
- 2 or 3 events at the same time. Options: smart, as locked (recommended) · side by side, always.
- A crowded spot. Options: side by side, a busy day gets wider, lanes below the minimum (recommended) · colour lanes + title list · side by side, equal days, titles wrap · keep cascading, titles on top · as now.
- Knobs (defaults): crowded from 4 at once · min column width 55 pt · widen busy day up to 2.5× · title lines in a narrow event 3 · time in narrow events hidden · hour height 60 · 7 days.
